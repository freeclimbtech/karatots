# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Parents of 3-5 year olds (flexible for a shy 6 year old) in the Bristol area researching a first structured activity for their child. They want confidence-building, discipline, physical activity, and a positive social environment, and they're weighing KARATOTS against other kids' clubs. They land on the site to judge whether it's safe, credible, and worth a free trial booking.

## Product Purpose

A 3-page marketing site for KARATOTS, the children's branch of Karate Bristol, run by Sensei David (David Judah Daniel, 6th Dan, Renshi). The site explains the offer, builds trust via the Karate Bristol connection, and converts parents into a free trial booking or enquiry via a contact form. Success = qualified parent enquiries.

## Brand Personality

Premium children's sports brand with traditional martial-arts credibility. Confident, warm, disciplined, welcoming — not aggressive, not cartoonish. Speaks to parents; makes karate sound exciting for kids without infantilising the design.

## Anti-references

Not a generic martial-arts template (no dragons, flames, clip-art, black/red gradients, stereotypical Asian imagery). Not a children's cartoon website. Not aggressive or fight-focused.

## Design Principles

- One clear conversion path: every section pushes toward booking a free trial.
- Trust before excitement: the Karate Bristol / Sensei David connection is established early and often.
- Ages 3-5 (flexible to 6) is the throughline — KARATOTS is the youngest entry point into the wider Karate Bristol journey.
- Restraint: a single red accent against charcoal/off-white, not a loud palette.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text), full keyboard navigation with visible focus states, semantic HTML and correct heading hierarchy, alt text on every image, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for scroll-reveal animation.
